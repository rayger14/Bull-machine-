## Evidence interface

Reuse same-source completed 1m/5m/15m/1h/4h/daily packets, causal witnesses,
precomputed indicative economics and the existing master-authored specialist
rulecards. Exclude their known educational examples and prior responses/outcomes.
Fibonacci fields with invalid/defaulted semantics cannot become confirmation.
Daily parent evidence is contextual; H3 is a separate hypothesis, not native
identity or a universal veto. No invented macro/derivatives observations.

observed zero and false, and provide explicit missing-input status records.

of atomic claims (detector, structure, sequence, economics, reason, missing),

formula, horizon, notional, round-trip cost, fill-known flag and deadline-exit

exact JSON schema, reference, ID table, packets/chunks and role instructions

## Fixed calls and pre-outcome gates

Fixed order: hourly-pass, hourly-reject, minute-pass, minute-reject; omit absent
strata. Each case gets one fresh assessor, then one fresh outcome-hidden semantic
reviewer; maximum eight role calls. Fresh-context capacity is a prerequisite:
do not reuse contaminated prior case contexts as a substitute. No retries,
response repair or case replacements. Existing exact ordered-chunk inner-return
capture is retained; it does not certify attention or enforced filesystem isolation.

Lock raw assessment/captures before reviewer construction. Reviewer gets its
case, raw answer, deterministic diagnostics, resolved claim evidence and fixed
reference, but no other case or future prices. Semantic review checks identity,
chronology, numerics, no invention and scope, plus each claim's evidence support
and plan completeness. Mechanical validation failure remains failure regardless
of reviewer leniency. Missing/invalid responses are recorded, not silently skips.
Lock every review/capture and grade before explicit outcome authorization.
