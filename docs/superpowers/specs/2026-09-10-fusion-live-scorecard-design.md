## Scores, thresholds and outcome definitions

Keep `fusion_score`, `threshold_at_entry`, `threshold_margin`, and `factor_attribution.entry_conditions.dynamic_threshold` separate. For a group, compute stored_score_minus_entry_threshold. Arithmetic consistency is absolute difference from logged margin <= 0.00015 + 1e-12, reflecting three independently rounded four-decimal fields. The designated entry threshold must be finite and >0 for the arithmetic-consistent analysis; threshold=0 is retained as sentinel/unknown, not threshold-cleared. Score must be finite in [0,1]; finite signed margin is allowed; threshold >1 remains an observed out-of-score-range threshold flag, not clipped. Display threshold missing/invalid is a coverage flag, not a reason to substitute it. Display threshold varying across exits is flagged. Do not reconstruct the decision score as threshold+margin or reverse an emergency penalty.

For arithmetic-consistent non-open groups, split the recorded `threshold_margin` at >=0 versus <0, labeled `logged_nonnegative_margin` and `logged_negative_margin`. Values within rounding tolerance of zero additionally flag `boundary_rounding_ambiguous`; report their counts, never claim exact historical pass/fail authority. All historical score stages/source versions remain unverified unless explicit row provenance is supplied; even explicit version labels are not authenticated. The arithmetic-consistent subset is not certified decision-stage evidence.

Outcome `recorded_exit_pnl_usd` is the sum of finite `pnl_usd` fields. If a row also has `pnl`, require agreement within 0.005 + 1e-12 or quarantine. Require positive quantities and entry prices. `quantity_sum` sums observed exits, not an asserted original quantity. A separate diagnostic risk proxy is `abs(entry_price - displayed_stop_loss) * quantity_sum`, only when the common displayed stop is positive and on the adverse side (long stop<entry, short stop>entry). This denominator is neither receipt-certified initial risk nor proof all exits were captured. Name the ratio `recorded_pnl_over_displayed_stop_risk_proxy`, never net R. Stop=0 is retained with no proxy. Financing/entry fees/cash movements are not reconstructed or subtracted speculatively.
