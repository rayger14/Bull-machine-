## Scope and evidence standard

Original X pages were inspected through the existing Chrome session. Search
fetching was insufficient; native browser accessibility text and screenshots
provided the observations below. No subscription barrier was bypassed and no
social action was taken. This is a source-index with paraphrases, not a saved
full-text archive, complete trader curriculum or independent verification of edge.
Dates are those displayed by X; intraday timezone was not established.

Source classes must remain separate: **what the author says; our interpretation;
the executable rule; measured results**. A verified account label is not validation
of a trading claim. Contemporary Wyckoff_Insider commentary is not an original
Richard Wyckoff primary source. Author assertions about universal algorithms,
consciousness, numerology or guaranteed gains are not established market facts.

## Original-source records

### WI01 — Fibonacci price ratios and time cycles

[Wyckoff_Insider, July 27, 2025](https://x.com/Wyckoff_Insider/status/1949423128217633091).
Opened original; selected sections 1–9 and the beginning of section 10 were read
visually because accessibility text truncated the long post. This is partial
coverage, not a complete transcription.

Section 3 explicitly lists 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786, 1.0, 1.618, 2.618 and 4.236.
Section 7 associates 1.618 with breakout extensions, 0.786 with pullbacks, 2.618
with continuation traps, and mentions Fibonacci time cycles. These establish
author-mentioned ratios and motifs. Inspected sections do not specify a causal
pivot detector, start/end anchor convention, timing units, tolerance, entry/stop
formula or evidence of incremental forecasting power.

**Correction:** older local notes broadly calling Fibonacci magnitudes project
inventions need qualification. Some ratios are demonstrably trader-mentioned;
our choice of anchors and executable use still needs separate justification.
This post does not establish 0.705 provenance, nor prove it was invented here.

### WI02 — Gann and temporal cycles, a separate source family

[Wyckoff_Insider, July 27, 2025](https://x.com/Wyckoff_Insider/status/1949399442873786607).
Opened original; screenshot passes covered sections 1–10 with small page-boundary
gaps, not a complete archived transcript.

The author discusses Gann's Square of 9, a 360-degree circle, and examples including
90/180 days, one year, seven/eight years and 144 months. The post mixes temporal
ideas with metaphysical interpretations. Its inspected content supplies no
reproducible conversion from confirmed BTC pivots to a trade decision.

Keep calendar/Gann hypotheses separate from Fibonacci bar-count projections.
Neither can justify a live timing boost merely because both discuss time.

### WI03 — Distinct timeframe roles and entry-time records

[Wyckoff_Insider, December 14, 2025](https://x.com/Wyckoff_Insider/status/2000223306620846408).
Opened original; full main-post text read visually. Attached folder illustration
was only partially visible and is not used for numerical claims.

The author organizes higher timeframes for analysis, middle timeframes for context,
and lower timeframes for execution. He recommends recording reasoning at entry,
then reviewing both trades taken and valid opportunities missed. This directly
supports distinct timeframe roles and an assessment recorded before outcome review.
It is workflow teaching, not an exact parent-range algorithm or universal demand
that every timeframe point in the same direction.

### B01/B02 — A context-dependent wick idea is not an entry signal

[Bojan, December 15, 2025 main post](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564037780946977)
and [same-author qualifying reply](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564041102901639).
The locally named handle `@Bojan_618` matched the original pages. Both short texts
were fully read in the original conversation. Chart thumbnail was visible but not
reconstructed; no exact chart prices are claimed.

Bojan describes fading a weekly open with an absent wick in a ranging environment,
while noting that trending/range-breakout conditions can leave that condition
unrepaired. His reply explicitly warns against an immediate short at the weekly
open: the anticipated move may arrive later, after an early entry is stopped out.
The reader still has to construct the trade.

This is especially useful for Bull Machine: a larger-timeframe attraction can
inform a hypothesis without determining entry time or invalidation. It does not
authenticate the engine's wick-anomaly thresholds, persistent-magnet machinery,
or a Fibonacci/time formula. It also does not establish that the existing
`wick_trap` archetype was specifically derived from this post.

## Implications for the master and specialists

These are project design inferences, not claims that the authors prescribed agents:

1. Preserve the larger-timeframe thesis separately from the lower-timeframe trigger.
   Record where the thesis stops applying; a destination is not entry permission.
2. Give every numerical price/time feature explicit anchors, units, confirmation
   clocks and tolerances. Teach the agent to distinguish a named ratio from a
   validated calculation. See the [code map](fibonacci_price_time_map_2026_09_12.md).
3. Freeze the reasoning and conditional plan before examining the outcome. Include
   missed opportunities as well as selected trades; otherwise a cautious agent
   can appear accurate simply by rejecting everything.

The earlier broad provenance verdict is qualified by WI01, not silently erased.
No source in this ledger validates a fusion cutoff, an archetype's expectancy,
or a new combined strategy. Past chart examples and published trade claims are
educational evidence, not an unbiased historical trading record.
