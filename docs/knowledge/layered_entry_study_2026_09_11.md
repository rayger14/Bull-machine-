# Layered entry study — twelve blinded cases and outcomes

## Current status

The expanded study is scored and independently reproduced. **All twelve fresh
assessors rejected their immediate entry: zero accepted, zero insufficient.**
Six rejected cases were profitable under the frozen hypothetical execution
contract and six lost. Three winners hit 2R; three exited profitably at the
deadline. This all-reject policy is economically identical to always skipping,
before unmeasured model costs. It demonstrates no useful entry selection or
profitable strategy. Final independent quant claim review is complete: no material
gate-scope error found; no demonstrated selection value or promotion approved.

The first source-preparation run exited before writing its artifact: the sticky
offline guard retained an optional urllib3 IPv6 capability-probe socket attempt
during constructor import. No network succeeded. Root reproduced the failure
with constructor-only input, then verified the existing native-adapter pattern
(`socket.has_ipv6=False` only inside the guarded scope) allows construction with
all17 archetypes, restores the flag, and still denies deliberate socket creation.
The completed rerun used isolated per-month processes/caches without changing selection,
data, engine rules or the guard. The three existing malformed example-YAML log
messages were retained; all17 actual archetypes were present. All three processes
exited successfully, the combined source artifact reproduced exactly, and all
twelve packet files reproduced unchanged before assessments.

The user approved broader dates and emphasized minute data inside larger
structure. The [registered protocol](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-layered-entry-study.md)
selected twelve cases: one per half-month per track in March, May and July
2026. These are alternating complete months within the live period, not selected
by profit. The archive has complete minute coverage and 30-day prior history for
all three; no claim that these calendar bins independently identify regimes.
The March/May/July population was already reused research before this study;
outcome-hidden assessment packets do not make it a pristine holdout.

Both hourly LC and minute sweep/reclaim receive completed 1m/5m/15m/1h/4h/daily

New `scripts/research/layered_entry_evidence.py` provides strict complete-bucket

## Pre-outcome safeguards and review

- Fresh isolated assessor per case, not multiple chronological cases in one
  context: later historical layers could otherwise reveal earlier outcomes.
- No native-emission masks, parent permission answers, future prices or
  performance-bearing code comments in packets. All responses lock before
  scoring. Prior revealed June packets are never retried.
- Unchanged scorer now consumes 1m bars for both tracks: 1440-minute hourly
  horizon /240-minute minute horizon, fixed stop/2R formula, independent $50k
  notional/$60 costs. Account equity unspecified; no portfolio, funding, impact
  or measured model-latency simulation.
- Hourly numeric gates define selection, so that comparator accepts every
  selected hourly case; agreement is not independent validation.
- Independent review caught numeric-string aggregation, a missing consistency
  assertion between minute sweep witness and parent-binding timestamp, parent
  configuration assumptions, and invalid-plan reporting. Corrections applied
  before historical packet freeze. Missing source evidence blocks the affected
  work; it does not authorize replacing a case.

Verification: fifteen new evidence tests failed before implementation and passed
afterward. Two additional numeric-input regressions were added during review,
one reproducing string-aggregation acceptance before correction. **416 research
tests pass**, with the existing LibreSSL warning. The private reporting function
was also exercised with an invalid-plan fixture; no invented PnL or crash.

## Coverage and fixed economics

All twelve calendar strata were populated: two hourly and two minute cases in
each of March, May and July 2026. All twelve had complete packets and valid
counterfactual plans; all twelve returned a conditional judgment; none was
accepted. All minute selections fall early on the first or fifteenth of the
month, an explicit consequence of the chronological sampling convention, not a
representative intramonth or session sample. This is not a full-period backtest.

Decision dates span March1–July15; final evaluated deadline is July16, 2026.
Starting equity is **unspecified**. Each hypothetical case independently uses
$50,000 notional, a fixed $60 round-trip cost, and a gross 2R target. Initial
stop risk excludes costs and is not account-equity risk. Average initial risk
across selected hourly cases is $818.78 (1.638% of notional); minute $116.83
(0.234%). The fixed cost is consequently much larger relative to minute risk.
Hourly horizon is 24 hours, minute four hours; both use 1m barrier ordering.
There are no invalid plans, missing tails or ambiguous both-hit bars in these
twelve outcomes. No book, compounding, funding, market impact or executable
model-delay allowance is represented.

## What the assessments do and do not establish

The packets now support explicit minute pivot/touch/sweep checks alongside
completed 5m/15m/hourly/4H/daily structure. All responses cite evidence IDs that
exist and keep hourly gates distinct from minute detector identity. Verified
absent algorithmic parents are not mislabeled as unavailable price history.
L06's phrase about an optional subtype's "universal eligibility" is unclear;
its rejection is explicitly contextual, not a hard-gate failure. These are
bounded packet/response observations, not certification of every narrative
support/resistance claim or a controlled proof that more layers improve models.

Agents commonly cite nearby resistance, unresolved minute selling or a wide
fixed stop. Those obstacles existed on successful paths too: L04 and L07 reached
their minute targets, and oversold hourly L06 reached its target despite the
agent's continuation-of-selling concern. Plausible explanation is not calibrated
probability. Review also identified a possible objective mismatch: repeatedly
demanding an unobstructed path to 2R is stricter than assessing expected net
payoff under a contract allowing profitable deadline exits. L02, L05 and L09
were profitable deadline exits. This does not establish that the prompt caused
the caution; it identifies something to clarify and test on new cases.
This design tests **immediate-entry veto**, not whether an agent can
predefine and then recognize a later confirmation entry. It does not test the
complete intended all-feed trader: no missing macro/derivatives observation was
invented, no agent-selected management was allowed, and model memory leakage
cannot be ruled out merely by excluding future packet data.

The previous June minute abstentions became contextual rejections here, but the
cases and prompts differ; this is not a same-case layer ablation. No reviewer
reran or repaired an assessor judgment after outcomes. The exact model snapshot,
token consumption and measured inference latency are not exposed by this run.
