# Trading concepts and translation rules

This register preserves meanings, not trading approval. `SRC-*` references resolve
in [sources](sources.md). Code statements concern the selected local route at
HEAD `85923a4` plus the existing uncommitted October repairs, not the remote server.
No row below certifies economic edge. Stable rule IDs are intended for later tests.

## RULE-01 Structure and timeframe roles

**Teaching:** analysis, context and execution serve different jobs; an HTF block
can contain an LTF range (SRC-WI-03/04/09, SRC-MT-02, SRC-ZI-01).
**Translation requirement:** identify each range/zone and its timeframe, then
record an explicit child-to-parent relationship and when both were available.
**Prohibited shortcut:** multiple high scores mean nested structure or all
timeframes must agree. **Current status:** directional context exists; actual
nested lineage is incomplete. **Recognition contrast:** identical scores with
different parents must not produce the same parent-backed permission. Chunk 4.

## RULE-02 Location exists before confirmation

**Teaching:** locate meaningful supply/demand before using an entry confirmation
(SRC-WI-01/03/04, SRC-ZI-01/04). A broad zone need not touch its finest refinement.
**Translation requirement:** retain named bounds, direction, formation and known-at
time; a sweep identifies the level breached and reclaimed, a retest identifies
the same surviving zone. **Prohibited shortcut:** any large wick, BOS-close
proximity or unrelated gap plus break is the complete setup.
**Current status:** native Sweep, OBR and FVG identities omit these relationships.
**Recognition contrast:** same candles at a known level versus no such level. Chunk 2.

## RULE-03 Interior and post-breakout tests are distinct

**Teaching:** an interior Model 2 LPS can substitute for a spring; post-SOS BU/LPS
tests former resistance (SRC-WI-02/05/06). **Translation requirement:** record
the structural role and referenced boundary before assigning an event label.
**Prohibited shortcut:** every LPS requires parent escape, or local strength is
parent escape. **Current status:** post-escape evidence is partial; interior M2
is context-only on the hourly route, full path off. **Recognition contrast:**
interior test retains its role without emitting a post-escape test. Chunks 1 and 4.

## RULE-04 A pullback develops before its turn confirms

**Teaching:** low-volume tests and staged response/acceptance differ from an
instant entry (SRC-WI-05, SRC-ZI-01/02/03). **Interpretation:** represent a
multi-candle episode rather than one initial quiet candle. **Convention under
review:** Chunk 1 specifies the aggregate, adverse extreme, turn, deadline and
failure clock. These exact choices are not supplied by the authors.
**Current status:** `_advance_test` still cancels a pending test when its initial
candle's adverse extreme is crossed. **Recognition contrast:** continuing retreat
with intact boundary versus actual boundary failure. Chunk 1.

## RULE-05 A local response does not clear the next barrier

**Teaching:** response, breakout, acceptance, retest and sustain are distinct;
local OB hold is not prior-swing-high reclaim (SRC-ZI-01/02/03/04).
**Translation requirement:** separate level IDs, events and governing candle
availability. **Prohibited shortcut:** a minute bounce proves a completed hourly
or daily setup. **Current status:** broad identity/clock linkage is incomplete.
**Recognition contrast:** early response with unclosed governing candle remains
developing. Chunks 2, 4 and 6.

## RULE-06 Fibonacci must name its measured object

**Teaching:** a weekly gap midpoint, a selected swing midpoint and HOB mitigation
depth are different quantities (SRC-MT-01/02, SRC-PH-02). WI supplies role-based
legs for different entries (SRC-WI-05/06). **Translation requirement:** store
object identity, directional endpoints, price basis, ratio and availability.
**Prohibited shortcut:** substitute a rolling-range midpoint for all three, or
assume older ratio lists complete newer negative-Fib instructions.
**Current status:** exact endpoint/negative-Fib construction remains unresolved.
**Recognition contrast:** equal numerical 0.5 values over different objects do
not become interchangeable evidence. Chunk 3.

## RULE-07 Extremum time differs from confirmation time

**Convention required for causality:** retain original pivot price/time separately
from when later candles confirm it. **Teaching boundary:** SRC-WI-07/08 discuss
ratios and time ideas but do not prescribe our clock algorithm. **Current defect:**
the inline Fib path shifts a pivot mask for confirmation, then reads price/time
at those shifted indices. **Prohibited shortcut:** fixing bookkeeping proves a
Gann or Fib forecasting advantage. **Recognition contrast:** an origin extreme
becomes usable later without moving to the confirmation candle. Chunk 3.

## RULE-10 A destination is not an entry signal

**Teaching:** a weekly wick objective may remain relevant after an early entry
fails; local response and larger thesis have separate conditions (SRC-BJ-01/02/03/04,
SRC-MT-04). **Interpretation:** crowding and macro observations likewise need a
separate executable policy; this extension is ours, not a quoted funding rule.
**Prohibited shortcut:** a persistent funding extreme or magnet permits repeated
entries by itself. **Current status:** Funding Divergence/Long Squeeze identify
crowding, OI's default structural identity is permissive. Chunk 6.

## RULE-11 Retest meaning depends on the setup

**Teaching:** an easy retest may challenge aggressive momentum, but a failure
then retest can be the intended short opportunity (SRC-CC-01/02).
**Translation requirement:** name the thesis and sequence before scoring a retest.
**Prohibited shortcut:** every retest adds bullish confidence, or invert a short
example into a fully sourced long rule. **Current status:** complete conditional
setup episodes remain missing in several native identities. Chunk 5.

## RULE-12 Identity must match the advertised setup

**Project semantic requirement, not recovered trader formula:** LC must define
which compression/reaction family it means; failed continuation must identify
what failed; breakout must identify its originating range. Do not retrofit
Wyckoff ancestry onto unrelated families. **Current status:** LC can recognize
climax/absorption or volume/RSI extremes without prior compression; Failed
Continuation uses FVG/ADX/volume ingredients. **Recognition contrast:** preserve
ingredients while removing the defining sequence. Each family gets its own
contract before implementation. Chunk 5.

## RULE-13 Historical confirmation is not permanent authority

**Teaching:** sustain/failure and risk adjustment matter (SRC-ZI-02/03/04,
SRC-AM-01). **Interpretation:** keep historical confirmation immutable while
current permission can expire or be revoked. **Translation requirement:** bind
evidence to parent, boundary and available-at time; invalidate consumers, not
only the display label. **Current status:** retained scores/parent lineage remain
partial; the existing same-parent phase-C sizing guard is already implemented.
**Recognition contrast:** a later failed/replaced parent cannot retain authority
through an old high score. Chunks 1 and 4; no broader sizing change authorized.
