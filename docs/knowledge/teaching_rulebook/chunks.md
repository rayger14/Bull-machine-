## Economic gate after one complete setup

Recognition acceptance is necessary but insufficient. Before evaluating returns,
freeze one isolated book's entry/management policy, costs, fill/latency assumptions,
data availability rules and chronological boundaries. Use minute data where needed
to resolve execution; report ambiguous intrabar order rather than inventing fills.
Evaluate coverage, rejected opportunities and drawdowns as well as winners.
Use walk-forward validation with appropriate overlap purging; CPCV only when the
event/sample design supports it. A new 2026 teaching applied to older history is
development, not an untouched holdout. Do not launch a model-assessor campaign or
change live orders/config merely because a mechanical recognition test passes.
