# Fixed-rule LC chronological extension — frozen before broad scoring

User approved the missed-winner diagnosis and larger code-only comparison.
This protocol extends the existing execution workflow, not the live strategy.

- Reuse all142 native long LC candidates from the verified31-month source census,
  January2024–July2026. Include previously exposed cases; label the whole study
  retrospective, not untouched holdout or walk-forward optimization. No resampling.
- Two independent single-position hourly-LC books: immediate and the existing
  prospective one-minute close above the last completed five-minute high.
- Freeze plans from predecision data: source close minus2.7 ATR14 stop,
  $50,000 notional, actual-entry-based2R target,15minute entry expiry, original
  decision+24hour deadline, zero routing delay. No new parameter search.
- Four scenarios:12/24bps round-trip costs crossed with90/300second processing.
  Primary12bps/90s. Record all scenarios, including unfavorable ones.
- Reconcile source hourly OHLC to the same minute archive. Missing/invalid source
  or predecision evidence remains an unavailable candidate, never removed or zero.
- Freeze chronological candidate IDs, plans, descriptive subtypes and input/code
  hashes before loading outcome windows. Verify hashes before/after scoring.
- Reuse the existing outcome resolver and independent occupancy books. No capital
  compounding, other-archetype interactions, funding, impact or live-fill claims.
- Report full books, fills, wins/losses, busy skips, expiry/cancellation, missing
  records, dollar net and primary dollar MTM drawdown. Group contributions by
  decision quarter/year and subtype without resetting occupancy at boundaries.
  Subtype groups are descriptive contributions, not separately executable books.
- Subtypes use the existing preflight geometry: close above prior hourly high
  means upside; close below prior low or sweep/reclaim means downside; otherwise
  unresolved. Labels are not gates. Check agreement on the18 frozen packet cases.
- Diagnose the exposed five missed winners against the five rejected downside
  losers using saved causal evidence and teaching/code mappings. Do not tune new
  gates or change targets/horizons to rescue their outcomes.
- Stop after one broad report and the diagnosis. No new market model calls,
  assessment retry, threshold optimization, deployment, or automatic paid campaign.
  A result dependent on a few trades/periods is not a dependable edge; disclose
  concentration, stability and small subgroup sizes instead of a pass certificate.

Deliverables: saved immutable broad inputs/results, tested small adapter,
source-backed diagnosis, chronological comparison and an actionable recommendation.
New work stays on the existing research branch. Preserve pinned old modules and
private artifacts. Publication/transfer is separate from local checkpoint commits.
