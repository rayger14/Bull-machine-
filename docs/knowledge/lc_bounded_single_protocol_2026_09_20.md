# Bounded single-assessor LC screen — frozen before market roles

User approved the bounded experiment on September20,2026. This is a retrospective
feasibility screen, not a trading promotion, pristine holdout, WFO or CPCV result.

## Question and sample

Does one context-aware agent preserve worthwhile LC entries while avoiding losses
better than immediate entry, mechanical confirmation and taking no trades?

Use the already prepared twenty-case January–July2026 roster, excluding exactly
Jan20 06:00 and Jan25 09:00 UTC, which were assessed and outcome-revealed. Preserve
all original packets, curriculum and old runtimes. Production preparation verifies
the original lock, the two prior grade/job bindings and all eighteen remaining
cases. No replacement, resampling, census rerun or outcome-based selection.
Remaining cases are `no_new_reveal_recorded`, not certified untouched history.

## Assessment and limits

- One fresh outcome-hidden specialist per case; requested Astra/high. Actual
  snapshot remains unknown unless independently observed. No shared conversation
  or developer handoff/outcome reports in specialist context.
- Maximum18 specialist launches, zero critics and zero retries. At most3 active.
  No additional market pilot. Software review is distinct from market assessment.
- Six hundred seconds per attempt, including packet delivery. Capture is timed
  from durable reservation to response-receipt processing in the same controller
  process. Restart loses timing continuity and yields an explicit null failure.
- This is a call ceiling, **not a guaranteed credit/dollar ceiling**. Billed credit
  telemetry is unavailable; do not invent measured spend or a provider hard cap.
- Exact unchanged published request/citation/menu validation. The wrapper adds
  the source-derived LC candidate subtype (descriptive, not an entry veto).
- Fixed choices: enter, wait for the published5m-high confirmation, reject, or
  uncertain/null. No free-form stop/target changes, new threshold or new memory.
- Preserve exact raw bytes and actual chunk-reader returns. Delivery validation
  is caller-attested, not model-attention or runtime identity authentication.
- Valid outputs are `schema_valid_unreviewed`, never critic-approved. Citation
  membership cannot prove semantic correctness; that limitation is reported.
- Every case needs a locked terminal before any new outcome read. Failures remain
  visible/null and cannot be reported as deliberate successful rejections.

## Deliverable and stop decision

One report: all eighteen decisions/reasons, subtype, reliability, latency, net
results, dollar MTM drawdown, winners preserved, missed winners, avoided losers,
and baseline losers still entered. Separate reject/expiry/cancellation/busy skips
and unavailable outcomes; differences cannot all be attributed to interpretation.
Case pairs and subtype labels remain available for descriptive inspection only.

Primary descriptive comparison is12bps/90s; practical viability also requires
the measured-agent-delay comparison. A positive total that exceeds both active
controls and reject-all would justify further investigation, not promotion or a
significance claim. Negative, all-reject, poor winner preservation, high invalidity
or latency-erased advantage means no move to live gating. Report every scenario
even when unfavorable. No rule tuning or second attempt on this sample.
