# Trader origins and archetype fidelity

*Scope: October 1, 2026; branch `quant/archetype-evidence-audit` at HEAD `85923a4`. This is a local selected-path audit, not a running-server fingerprint or historical replay.*

**Bull Machine contains real, useful implementations of Wyckoff context, price structure, liquidity, Fibonacci proximity and derivatives state, but its current 17 archetypes are not faithful executable versions of 17 original trader setups.** The selected path computes hierarchical 1D, 4H and 1H Wyckoff evidence, and every enabled archetype gives that evidence a positive fusion weight. Yet no executable identity requires a named Wyckoff range and lifecycle; most recognize ingredients such as a wick, RSI extreme, FVG flag or funding state, then enter at the current close with common ATR-based risk and central management. Direct temporal gates appear in two setups; the separate Fib premium flag can affect fusion for every long. Hidden extensions and source-described Gann calendar timing do not govern champion entries.

The combined hypothesis should therefore be narrowed: use Wyckoff-style supply, demand and sequence as contextual structure where the setup calls for it; keep price location, time windows and Gann ideas as separate, source-versioned hypotheses; and never let a target or contextual score stand in for an entry trigger. This audit establishes source and implementation fidelity only. It does not show that a faithful version would be profitable, explain prior losses, or justify rewriting all 17.

## Original teachers converge on context and sequence, not one formula

The bounded original-source review resolved five of the six founding teachers: **Wyckoff Insider, Moneytaur, Bojan, IamZeroIka and Crypto Chase**. Phoenix remains an unresolved founding name: the inspected repository supplies no defensible handle, original link or unique teaching. The fresh ledger covers **11 grouped teaching items across 12 top-level URLs**—ten X statuses and two author-channel videos. The Wyckoff Insider, Moneytaur and Bojan main texts were freshly read; one ZeroIka thread was read only through items 1–6 of 25, while his second main post was complete; both Crypto Chase sources were read through complete auto-caption transcripts, with time-coded chart narration but without frame-by-frame reconstruction. Two Adam Mancini articles were read from saved local text, not the web; they are later ES-transfer material with no canonical article URLs or chart archive, not founding ancestry. `@TheAstronomer` remains a separate code-only attribution, not a safe alias for Phoenix. ([bounded source ledger](../research_notes/Trader%20origins%20and%20archetype%20fidelity/original_trader_sources.md))

The strongest shared lesson is ordinary but operationally demanding: **identify the level and the timeframe that gives the idea meaning, wait for appropriate evidence, and keep destination separate from entry permission**. Moneytaur says to wait at known levels, distinguishes a wick through a level from candle bodies closing beyond it, and asks traders to pre-plan liquidity or take-profit destinations; he does not explain how to construct those key levels or a Fib anchor. ([Moneytaur execution post](https://x.com/Moneytaur_/status/1819660778359644193)) Bojan presents an unwicked weekly open as a possible attraction in a bracketing market, then explicitly warns that this does not authorize an immediate short and can stop an early trader before the repair occurs. ([Bojan main post](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564037780946977), [qualifying reply](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564041102901639)) ZeroIka places price action and fundamentals before indicators and includes HH/HL–LH/LL, supply and demand, OBs, FVGs, Fib premium/discount and derivatives as supporting vocabulary, not one exclusive method. ([IamZeroIka main post](https://x.com/IamZeroIka/status/1779921797434982462))

Crypto Chase makes the timeframe distinction especially concrete. His daily-demand examples judge acceptance and invalidation on daily closes; lower-timeframe weakness does not automatically invalidate a daily thesis, and waiting for the defining close can still end in a stop. ([acceptance example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRMikMIlOwM&t=122s), [timeframe qualifier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRMikMIlOwM&t=392s)) His other lesson uses daily or 4H charts for direction and 1H/15m/5m for execution, with one confluence example combining daily support/resistance, a `.618–.705` retracement and break-origin support. It is an example, not a universal anchor algorithm. ([timeframe roles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ouxW_yUISgs&t=25s), [Fib example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ouxW_yUISgs&t=201s))

These sources motivate four separate clocks and controls: a **context timeframe** for the market thesis, an **entry timeframe** for execution evidence, a **thesis-invalidation timeframe** for deciding whether the idea failed, and a **hard risk stop** that limits loss before the thesis can be leisurely reassessed. Keeping them separate is a source-informed architecture recommendation, not a universal rule recovered verbatim from every teacher. The two archived Mancini records reinforce the same general sequence—significant level, loss and recovery, structural acceptance, stop beyond the whole structure, level-to-level scaling and a small runner—but only for the saved ES examples. ([Mancini archive text](</Users/rayghandchi/Mancini bot/Mancini/data/substack/all_posts.json>))

([Wyckoff scan](https://archive.org/details/studiesintaperea00wyckrich), [OCR](https://archive.org/stream/studiesintaperea00wyckrich/studiesintaperea00wyckrich_djvu.txt)) A–E, spring/test, SOS and LPS are supported here by a modern Wyckoff-school synthesis. ## Wyckoff runs live but does not govern setup identity

The current selected chain is the paper service using `configs/champion_paper.json`, then Coinbase runner → live feature computer → V11 runner → isolated archetype engine → `StructuralChecker`, which imports `engine/archetypes/logic.py`. ([service selection](../deploy/coinbase-paper.service#L14), [structural import](../engine/archetypes/structural_check.py#L25)) This matters because richer `logic_v2_adapter`, `domain_fusion`, knowledge hooks and Bojan/HOB components exist elsewhere but do not define the selected champion path. S9/HOB is callable, for example, but no enabled immediate `hob_reaction.yaml` instantiates it in the current 17. ([S9 mapping](../engine/archetypes/structural_check.py#L47), [S9 logic](../engine/archetypes/logic.py#L1183))

Wyckoff itself is **active and substantive**. The live producer detects SC, BC, AR, AS, ST, SOS, SOW, Spring, UT, UTAD, LPS and LPSY, runs 1D→4H→1H context, and supplies direction-aware scores. ([live Wyckoff producer](../bin/live/live_feature_computer.py#L1604), [daily context](../bin/live/live_feature_computer.py#L1796)) The archetype consumer combines bullish or bearish 1H/4H/1D scores, so a Wyckoff event can affect actual entry permission through fusion, and a C-accumulation phase can increase the size of any long. ([directional fusion](../engine/archetypes/archetype_instance.py#L384), [size boost](../bin/live/v11_shadow_runner.py#L1454)) The fidelity loss occurs after detection: recent event scores are carried forward as scalar maxima, while most identities do not require the parent range, boundary, event ID, order, freshness or invalidation that made the event meaningful.

The phrase “fusion is bypassed” is only half true. Every candidate first passes structure, cooldown and YAML gates, computes fusion, and must clear its archetype's **inner YAML fusion threshold** in the default fusion mode. ([entry order and inner floor](../engine/archetypes/archetype_instance.py#L798), [fusion threshold](../engine/archetypes/archetype_instance.py#L840)) The current `bypass_threshold=true` setting bypasses the runner's later **outer dynamic threshold**, not the inner floor. Below that outer threshold, failed soft gates are normally blocked again; above it, a soft-gate failure can survive, with Confluence Breakout opting out of enforcement under bypass. ([bypass setting](../configs/champion_paper.json#L124), [outer branch](../bin/live/v11_shadow_runner.py#L1168), [CB opt-out](../configs/champion/archetypes_v14rq/confluence_breakout.yaml#L77)) Thus Wyckoff can influence permission and size, but other domains can compensate for it; there is no minimum Wyckoff score or required lifecycle. This is the behavior of the inspected checkout, not proof of what every historical replay or unverified server process actually consumed.

([alternate Fib levels](../engine/liquidity/fib_levels.py#L17))

Only `order_block_retest` and `retest_cluster` directly consume temporal fields. OBR soft-gates an inline price-time score. ([OBR gates](../configs/champion/archetypes_v14rq/order_block_retest.yaml#L21), [Retest Cluster identity](../engine/archetypes/logic.py#L834), [Retest Cluster gates](../configs/champion/archetypes_v14rq/retest_cluster.yaml#L23)) The implementation has concrete semantic contradictions: its centered pivot mask is shifted to wait for confirmation, which is availability-safe, but it then reads price and starts the time count at the **confirmation timestamp rather than the original extremum**. The active temporal scalar's “Gann” contribution is proximity to a **price multiple of nine**, not a time forecast. That conclusion rejects neither elapsed time as a research variable nor Gann's historical hypothesis; it rejects calling the current mechanics equivalent.

## The next step is one source-locked contract, not seventeen rewrites

Three decision classes must remain separate. **Demonstrated semantic contradictions** include oldest-versus-last pivot age, confirmation-time versus origin-time Fib anchoring, unreachable configured time counts, a discarded batch temporal score, and central exit defaults that do not consume per-YAML exit blocks. These warrant specification review, but the findings do not select a profitable replacement. **Proposed architecture and policy choices** include preserving parent range, level and event IDs through entry; defining an active-leg and lifecycle for extensions; giving neutral archetypes executable sides; making OI require a divergence object; deciding whether structural errors should fail closed; and binding thesis invalidation to management. Those may improve fidelity, but they are not all software bugs and were not recovered as universal author rules. **Economic claims**—that Wyckoff sequence gates, Fib location, Fib time, or Gann calendar windows improve expectancy—remain untested here.

The finite next action is to choose **one setup**, lock a bounded set of original sources and chart examples, and write one versioned contract covering context timeframe, parent/range or level ID, origin and availability clocks, ordered transitions, entry timeframe, cancellation, thesis invalidation, hard risk stop, destination and conditional management. Then trace that unchanged contract through producer, feature row, identity, gates, fusion, entry and exit before any economic comparison. Existing detectors and causal ledgers are reuse candidates, not proof of active fidelity. Any worked contract under the current scope should attach to R1, R3 or the unchanged Liquidity Compression benchmark; additional sequence definitions must be labeled proposed project choices, not author-endorsed rules. R1 and R3 remain unimplemented proposals; R2 remains parked; prior Liquidity Compression no-promotion evidence remains unchanged. ([current repair scorecard](../docs/knowledge/archetype_repair_scorecard_2026_09_30.md#L198)) This is deliberately narrower than broad parameter search or 17 simultaneous repairs.

## Conclusion

The research changes the central diagnosis. Bull Machine does not lack trading concepts; it loses setup meaning as rich context becomes a score, flag or same-bar condition. Its strongest live achievement is genuine multi-timeframe Wyckoff context. Its largest fidelity gap is that context rarely survives as the persistent, directional object that defines entry, expiry and invalidation.

That makes the revised hypothesis both more modest and more testable: preserve structure and clocks first, treat price and time tools as separate optional claims, and ask economics only after the intended setup can be recognized without hindsight. Source authenticity can improve representation. It cannot turn numerology into evidence or guarantee an edge.
