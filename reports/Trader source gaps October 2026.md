# Original trader charts expose missing relationships

**Bull Machine has many of the right ingredients, but its 17 archetypes still lose relationships that make an original trader setup meaningful:** which range contains the pattern, which level is being tested, which timeframe confirms it, and when that evidence stops authorizing action. The October 5 original-X pass closes specific text, chart and identity gaps; it does not recover complete curricula or certify trading edge. The strongest additions separate an interior Wyckoff Model 2 LPS from a later parent-range escape, a broad zone from optional refinement, and a gap midpoint from a swing midpoint. They also show why a retest can support one playbook while challenging another. Recent local range and phase-sizing repairs are real partial progress, but complete pullback recognition and nested lineage remain unfinished. The next deliverable is one source-reviewed developing-pullback contract with fresh mirrored contrasts, before further implementation or economics.
