FUNDING MECHANICS (documented precisely, load-bearing for Form A PnL):
  Funding is a PAID cash flow between longs and shorts, exchanged every 8h.
  If funding_rate > 0: LONGS PAY SHORTS.   If funding_rate < 0: SHORTS PAY LONGS.
  Payment per settlement = funding_rate * position_notional.
  Cash flow to a position: cf = -side * funding_rate * notional
    side = +1 long, -1 short. (Short with rate>0 RECEIVES +rate*notional; long with
    rate<0 RECEIVES -rate*notional = +|rate|*notional.) Modeled EXPLICITLY every 8h.
  Binance 8h cadence => 3 settlements/day => annualization factor = 3*365 = 1095.

FORM A — FUNDING-EXTREME FADE (standalone carry strategy; the primary deliverable)
=====================================================================================
Grid: the 8h funding-settlement grid (timestamps 00/08/16 UTC). BTC 1H resampled to
  8h OHLC aligned to those boundaries. All decisions at 8h closes; stop checked using
  the 8h bar low/high; funding accrues at each held 8h settlement.

SIGNAL (all causal):
  f3 = trailing 3-DAY MEAN of the 8h funding rate = rolling mean of the last 9
       settled 8h rates (3 days * 3/day). Causal (uses t and priors only).
  Expanding-causal percentiles of the f3 SERIES: at each step t, P85_t / P50_t /
       P15_t = 85th/50th/15th percentile of {f3_s : s <= t} (expanding window,
       recomputed each step, uses only past+present). Warmup: no trades until
       >= 270 completed 8h steps of f3 history (~90 days) so percentiles are defined.

ENTRY (one position at a time; carry book, no pyramiding):
  If FLAT and f3_t > P85_t  -> SHORT (crowded longs pay us; we collect + funding).
  If FLAT and f3_t < P15_t  -> LONG  (crowded shorts pay us; we collect - funding).
  (Symmetric mirror of the 85th extreme; 15th is the pre-registered lower extreme.)

SIZE (carry, wide stop):
  risk_dollars = 0.01 * equity (1% risk, fixed-fractional on running equity).
  stop_distance = 3.0 * ATR   (ATR = Wilder ATR14 on the DAILY frame, value as of the
       entry day; wide because this is a carry hold, not a breakout).
  qty = risk_dollars / stop_distance ; notional = qty * entry_price.

HOLD / FUNDING ACCRUAL:
  Each held 8h settlement: funding_cf = -side * funding_rate(t) * notional (added to PnL).
  (notional marked at entry price, held constant — conservative; funding is on position size.)

EXIT (first to trigger):
  (a) STOP: short stops if 8h-bar HIGH >= entry + 3*ATR; long stops if 8h-bar LOW <=
      entry - 3*ATR. Fill at the stop price. (price leg = -risk_dollars by construction.)
  (b) NORMALIZATION: short exits when f3_t < P50_t; long exits when f3_t > P50_t.
      Fill at the 8h close.
  No take-profit (a carry position is held for the cash flow, exited on normalization/stop).
  No max-hold cap (spec says "hold while the extreme persists").

COSTS: 2bps commission + 3bps slippage = 5bps per side, on notional, at entry and exit.

PnL DECOMPOSITION (reported): price_leg + funding_leg - costs = net, per trade and book.
  gross_profit = sum of positive-net trades; gross_loss = sum of |negative-net trades|.
  PF = gross_profit / gross_loss.

PARITY (referee): independent re-computation of book net PnL two ways
  (per-trade sum vs equity-curve delta) MUST agree to 0.00%.

CAUSALITY 3-POINT CHECK: on f3 and on each expanding percentile series, verify that the
  value at index k is unchanged when the series is truncated at k (no future leakage /
  no repaint), at 3 sampled indices. MUST be 0 mismatches.

PRE-REGISTERED VALIDATION RULE (Form A validates iff ALL THREE):
  A1  total net PnL > 0 AND PF >= 1.30
  A2  funding_leg cash flow >= 40% of GROSS PROFIT (proves it is CARRY, not disguised
      mean-reversion price luck)
  A3  no single squeeze EPISODE draws the book down more than -15% (book DD within episode)
  If any fail: Form A does NOT validate — reported plainly as thin/squeeze-fragile.

REQUIRED HONEST REPORTING (regardless of pass/fail):
  - annualized carry YIELD captured (funding_leg / avg_notional / years, annualized)
  - benchmark vs FLAT CASH (0% book) and the short-vol honesty framing
  - SQUEEZE-EPISODE TABLE: worst adverse episodes for the short-collecting book, esp.
    2021 bull squeezes (shorts-collecting-funding run over — the known death mode),
    per episode: dates, book DD, funding collected, price loss, net.
  - per-YEAR / era stratification (2020-22 vs 2023-26).
