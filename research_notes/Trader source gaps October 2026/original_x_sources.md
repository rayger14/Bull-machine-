## What do the original Wyckoff posts add about location, parent ranges, and confirmation?

### Takeaway

Fresh signed-in Chrome readings on 2026-10-05 distinguish a setup inside a larger range from a separate escape of that range, and distinguish a broad supply/demand range from refined zones within it. ## What do Zero Ika and Crypto Chase originals establish about tests and invalidation?

### Takeaway

Zero Ika's original lesson treats response, local absorption, repeated tests, breakout, retest, sustain and invalidation as different stages, with different timeframe requirements. Crypto Chase supplies a setup-dependent qualification: an easy retest can undermine an aggressive momentum-breakout thesis, while a retest after a support failure can be the intended short opportunity. [Lesson](https://x.com/IamZeroIka/status/1962520664436621597), [steps 5–8](https://x.com/IamZeroIka/status/1962520668949328027), [steps 9–12](https://x.com/IamZeroIka/status/1962520672661291044), [Chase momentum case](https://x.com/Crypto_Chase/status/1828376670761570758), [Chase failure/retest case](https://x.com/Crypto_Chase/status/1905514314980618310)
