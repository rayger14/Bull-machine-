## How should the bounded implementation and validation proceed later?

### Takeaway

Use incremental contract repair, followed by one complete archetype under separate approval. The present finish line is the ranked backlog, operational source ledger and recommended recognition-test plan (not a frozen case packet); it does not include another backtest or implementation. [Current approved next-action boundary](../../docs/knowledge/wyckoff_range_repair_2026_10_05.md#next-deliverable)

### Cited Findings

Hard gates reject while soft gates reduce fusion proportionally; missing values with skip/pass can remove checks from the denominator. Thus source-defining identity should be distinguishable from optional corroboration. The newer phase-C sizing guard already requires current confirmed same-parent spring-test evidence; phase C alone no longer grants the 1.25 multiplier. [Gate handling](../../engine/archetypes/archetype_instance.py#L702), [current guard](../../engine/wyckoff/range_evidence.py#L288)

### Inferences

Three approaches have fair tradeoffs. A coordinated 17-archetype rewrite could standardize provenance and remove duplication, but has large integration surface and no uniformly complete definitions. Adding fusion weights is cheap and may be useful for later calibrated contextual ranking, but cannot reconstruct absent level identity or event order. Incremental repair uses the existing range evidence to close one verified semantic gap, adds only needed shared observations, and proves one consumer at a time; it progresses more slowly across the roster but yields inspectable causal contracts. Recommend the third.

Next freeze a source-only pullback contract and exactly twelve fresh raw-OHLCV contrasts, balanced between accumulation/distribution and genuine/lookalike/ambiguous examples. Labels and required milestones precede execution; include prefix/future-tail comparisons and availability clocks. Ambiguous cases should retain uncertainty rather than be forced into positive/negative classes. Preserve the old exposed twelve as regression only. Acceptance means correct object/role, confirmation timing, withheld permission during development, real failure/expiry and cancelled consumer authority; passing a copied predicate is insufficient.

Then, under separate implementation approval, complete one specifically chosen archetype from identity through permission, entry, invalidation and management. Source-defining requirements are mandatory within that archetype; volume, momentum, derivatives and HTF context need not all agree. Keep separate archetype books. Shared observations may be reused, but one event is not seventeen independent votes; portfolio arbitration follows isolated attribution.

Only after recognition acceptance use minute timing, next-available prices, fees, spread/slippage, latency, funding, stops/targets and collisions. Freeze policies and chronological boundaries before outcomes; apply economic walk-forward work to the approved book. Applying 2026 source-known definitions to older history is development, not a virgin holdout. Selected author charts are not profit evidence. CPCV needs appropriate overlap/leakage design and sufficient events. A source-faithful setup may fail economics; a profitable proxy may lack the advertised identity. Report both separately.
