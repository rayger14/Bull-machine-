## What do the preserved original-page receipts support about context, level, sequence, entry, invalidation and management?

### Takeaway

The receipts support three narrow teaching families: Moneytaur's location/confirmation/destination discipline, Wyckoff Insider's separated timeframe roles plus named Fibonacci motifs, and Bojan's regime-qualified wick attraction with an explicit warning against immediate entry. They do not recover complete executable strategies.

### Cited Findings

- **Moneytaur:** M01 supports waiting at already identified levels, choosing confirmation appropriate to the execution horizon, distinguishing wicks from sustained bodies beyond a level, and identifying a target/liquidity destination inside the relevant range before entry. When no clear target exists, the receipt reports avoidance or a distinct momentum/trailing approach. That supports context, level, ordered confirmation, destination and management categories; it does not provide a universal invalidation or stop formula. M02 adds only the author's interpretation that higher-timeframe candle bodies carry more liquidity/reversal pressure than lower-timeframe bodies; the full post was not read ([M01/M02 receipt](../../docs/knowledge/moneytaur_primary_sources_2026_09_12.md)).
- **Wyckoff Insider:** WI01 names ratios including 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786, 1.0, 1.618, 2.618 and 4.236 and associates some with pullbacks, breakout extensions and continuation traps. It supplies no causal pivot detector, anchor convention, tolerance, entry, stop or demonstrated forecasting increment. WI03 assigns higher timeframes to analysis, middle timeframes to context and lower timeframes to execution, then recommends recording entry reasoning and reviewing taken and missed valid opportunities. This supports role separation and pre-outcome recordkeeping, not mandatory all-timeframe agreement or a parent-range algorithm ([WI01/WI03 ledger](../../docs/knowledge/trader_primary_source_ledger_2026_09_12.md)).
- **Bojan:** The main/reply pair frames an absent weekly-open wick as a possible attraction in a ranging environment, while warning that trends or range breakouts can leave it unrepaired. The reply warns against shorting immediately at the weekly open because the anticipated move can occur only after an early entry is stopped. Thus context precedes the wick hypothesis, and the destination does not supply entry timing; the reader must still define confirmation, invalidation and management ([B01/B02 ledger](../../docs/knowledge/trader_primary_source_ledger_2026_09_12.md)).

### Inferences

- A faithful project translation would store the pre-existing level or attraction, applicable regime and timeframe, observable confirmation sequence, thesis failure condition, destination and management policy as separate fields. This is an engineering inference, not wording prescribed by the traders.

### Gaps

- None of these six sources authenticates exact zone construction, pivot anchors, tolerances, stop placement, sizing, expiry, scale-out percentages, expected value, or profitability.
