## What is source-supported about price, time, sequence and management?

### Takeaway

The originals support named motifs and disciplined sequencing, not the project's precise geometry. The most operational original is Crypto Chase's timeframe-matched acceptance/invalidation lesson; WI/Moneytaur do not provide the missing Fib/Gann calculation contract.

### Cited Findings

- **Price location:** WI01 names ratios but not anchors; M03 names hidden levels without construction; CC02 shows a `.618–.705` confluence example but not a universal pivot algorithm. — [WI01](https://x.com/Wyckoff_Insider/status/1949423128217633091); [M03](https://x.com/Moneytaur_/status/1717106022265897019); [CC02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ouxW_yUISgs)
- **Time:** WI01 merely says Fib time cycles; WI02 lists calendar/numerological periods but no start, reset or tolerance. — [WI01](https://x.com/Wyckoff_Insider/status/1949423128217633091); [WI02](https://x.com/Wyckoff_Insider/status/1949399442873786607)
- **Sequence:** Moneytaur supplies level → wait → wick/body confirmation → destination; Bojan supplies range context → possible weekly-open attraction but requires independently structured entry; Crypto Chase supplies source-timeframe level → close-based acceptance/rejection → hold or invalidate. — [M01](https://x.com/Moneytaur_/status/1819660778359644193); [B01/B02](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564037780946977); [CC01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRMikMIlOwM)
- **Management:** M01 asks for clear TP/liquidity levels and distinguishes avoidance from momentum/trailing treatment; CC01 presents actual partial exits and an invalidation exit after a bounce, while warning that waiting for the defining timeframe can worsen realized loss. — [M01](https://x.com/Moneytaur_/status/1819660778359644193); [CC01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRMikMIlOwM)
- Project formulas such as 2.5% Fib tolerances, timeframe multipliers and volume bonuses are explicit code choices, despite a “Moneytaur post:10” comment. — [hidden_fibs.py](../../bull_machine/strategy/hidden_fibs.py#L157)

### Inferences

- A faithful research contract must record level/range ID, anchor prices and confirmation time, direction, originating timeframe, acceptance clock, expiry/reset and invalidation. Ratio presence alone cannot supply those fields.
- The originals justify testing contextual features; they do not justify calling those features predictive or profitable.

### Gaps

- No source inspected specifies a complete price-and-time intersection rule, tolerance calibration, timezone/session convention or causal validation protocol.
