## What recognition-edge contrasts do the accessible teachings support?

### Takeaway

The clearest cross-source contrast is that a level touch, wick, sweep or destination is not itself acceptance or an entry. A recognition card should preserve the level and originating timeframe, then test close/hold behavior and local structural confirmation without turning HTF disagreement into an automatic universal veto.

### Cited Findings

- **Fake reclaim versus supported reclaim.** Z01’s copied example combines a deviation below the daily 9 EMA with a buyback/reclaim **and** an intact higher low inside an HH/HL posture; the same thread tells a long-only reader to wait when LH/LL and below-EMA conditions prevail. A card showing only a wick below and close back above, but a broken prior low or LH/LL context, is therefore a near-miss rather than the mirrored positive example. — [Z01 mirror](https://en.rattibha.com/thread/1623715383218929666)
- **Sweep versus break-and-acceptance.** Zero Ika’s 2024-05-03 copied index item offers bearish continuation either by retest or by a liquidity sweep/spike into a bearish order block; it says only HTF closes above the specified structural threshold would change posture. This is a direct counterexample to labeling a liquidity run itself as a bullish break. The exact source post and chart remain unavailable. — [author index, lower-confidence copied text](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/IamZeroIka)
- **Initial reaction versus confirmed hold.** The 2025-09-01 copied item starts from an already identified HTF demand/breaker area. It calls a 1-minute response plus 1-hour close/absorbing volume “early” and “missing confirmation,” then asks for a local-swing break or LTF CHoCH plus retest; later steps require the flipped area to sustain and describe a stop below a key line. Thus a touch, volume spike or first close is not interchangeable with confirmed acceptance. — [author index, lower-confidence copied text](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/IamZeroIka)
- **Failed retest/invalidation.** The same copied sequence treats a resistance-to-support flip, successful retest, consolidation above the swing high and continued hold of a green area as distinct observations. An exam case that breaks above but closes back through the claimed support, or never produces the retest/hold, should be labeled failed/unfinished rather than silently accepted. The author index does not provide a general failure clock or universal stop formula. — [author index, lower-confidence copied text](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/IamZeroIka)
- **Level location precedes trigger.** Z02’s copied main text names supply/demand zones and order blocks, closures around key areas, and candle bodies/wicks; the 2025 copied item begins with a “solid demand or breaker area” before examining LTF behavior. A structurally similar sweep in the middle of nowhere should not be treated as the same setup. — [Z02 mirror](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1779921797434982462.html); [2025 author index](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/IamZeroIka)
- **HTF/LTF roles are unequal but not a blanket veto.** Z02 says its concepts carry more weight on HTFs. The 2025 copied example uses an HTF level, asks for a decent close on 1H/4H to judge whether it holds, and uses LTF to observe real-time confirmation; it says entry after only the 1H evidence carries higher risk. This supports explicit role/conflict labeling, not “all timeframes must agree or reject.” — [Z02 mirror](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1779921797434982462.html); [2025 author index](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/IamZeroIka)
- **Destination is not permission.** Subject to the older-receipt limitation, Bojan’s paired statuses say a range-dependent weekly-open attraction may remain unfilled in a trend/breakout and warn that immediate entry can be stopped before the anticipated repair. An examiner should reject “magnet exists, therefore enter now.” — [B01](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564037780946977); [B02](https://x.com/Bojan_618/status/2000564041102901639)
- **Crypto Chase locator contrast.** The secondary indexed excerpts consistently distinguish a sweep or repeated attempt from close-based acceptance/flip and describe time spent beyond a boundary as thesis damage. They are useful for finding the originals, but absent exact status IDs they should not be the sole factual basis of a scored card. — [2022 locator](https://marginatm.com/bitcoin-ve-dau-neu-fed-tang-lai-suat-0bjmzzkn); [2023 locator](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph%3Ad2220eee9094b%3A0-bitcoin-breakout-matter-of-time-says-analysis-with-btc-price-at-28k/); [2025 locator](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoin-fully-gassed-leave-100k-btc-price-behind-analysis)

### Inferences

- Minimal recognition state should keep separate fields for: pre-existing level/zone identity and timeframe; excursion/sweep; source-timeframe close/acceptance; local break/CHoCH; retest/hold; failure boundary; expiry/unknown. Collapsing these into one bullish/bearish score erases the exact contrasts above.
- The closest Wyckoff-style mapping is contextual, not terminological: a spring-like undercut without reclaim/acceptance and confirmation is a candidate only; acceptance without preserved structure is suspect; a break without retest/hold may be unfinished; an attraction without entry evidence is not a trigger.

### Gaps

- None of the freshly accessible material defines a universal bar count, percentage penetration, volume multiple, ATR buffer, retest deadline or phase label. Those remain project choices.
- “Failed retest” is an exam inference from the copied hold/retest sequence, not a verbatim universal rule from the authors.
- The accessible texts do not establish profitability, frequency, cross-market transfer or that these examples are canonical Wyckoff springs/UTADs.

## How should these sources change the recognition exam?

### Takeaway

Use the sources to sharpen semantic contrasts, not to import numerical rules. The highest-value cases vary one causal distinction at a time and preserve unknowns when the chart/source evidence is missing.
